Bharat Blocks Privacy Policy

Sarathkumar.K · Effective date: 30 October 2026

Summary

  • Bharat Blocks is a general puzzle game for ages 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. You do not need an account, a login, an email address or a phone number to play.
  • The game does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.
  • Your progress, settings and chosen language are saved only on your device.
  • The game needs an internet connection to play. About every 5 seconds while it is open, it sends a small HTTPS request to a Google connectivity-check address, and it pauses while the device is offline. This check sends no personal data.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. As a conservative setting, ads are still configured as child-directed, non-personalised and limited to a G (general audience) rating. The game does not collect the Android Advertising ID. The Google ad SDK does process some device data to deliver ads (see section 4).
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data for advertising profiles.

1. Who we are

Bharat Blocks (Android package com.bharat.blocks) is developed and published by Sarathkumar.K ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at sarathalapad@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps the following on your device only:

  • Scores: Classic high score, Rush 100s best score, and Daily Challenge best score and streak.
  • Adventure progress (levels and regions unlocked, stars) and any board still in progress.
  • Coins, and the block skins you own and have selected.
  • Settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion, graphics quality) and your chosen language.
  • Whether the tutorial is finished, and a count of game launches, used only to skip the app-open ad on the very first launch after install.

This data is never sent to us or to any server, and it is excluded from Android device backups. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it.

3. Internet connection check

Bharat Blocks requires an internet connection. To find out whether the device is online, the game sends an HTTPS HEAD request (headers only, with no message body) to https://clients3.google.com/generate_204. If that does not answer, it tries https://connectivitycheck.gstatic.com/generate_204. Both are public connectivity-check addresses run by Google.

  • When: at launch, about every 5 seconds while the game is open on screen, and straight away when you return to the game or tap Retry. The game makes no checks while it is in the background, or while Android reports no network at all.
  • After a failed check: the game checks again after 2 seconds. While the offline screen is showing, checks slow down step by step, to one every 30 seconds at most.
  • If there is no connection: the game pauses and asks you to reconnect, and resumes when the connection is back.

This request sends no personal data: no name, no account, no Advertising ID, no other identifier from the game and no game data. Like any internet request, the Google server that answers it receives the device's IP address and basic technical request information (such as the software version in the request headers). The game uses the result only to decide whether the device is online, and does not store it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bharat Blocks is free and is supported by ads delivered through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, by Google LLC). No other ad network or mediation SDK is used. The game shows a banner at the bottom of every screen, an app-open ad at launch (except the very first launch after install), a full-screen ad after a finished run or level, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a revive or double coins.

How ads are configured

Bharat Blocks is for ages 13 and over. Even so, we keep a conservative ad setup for all players:

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA "tag for child-directed treatment") and as under the age of consent, and are non-personalised.
  • The maximum ad content rating is set to G (general audiences) on every ad request.
  • The game removes the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission, so the Advertising ID is not collected.

What the ad SDK may process

To deliver ads, count impressions and prevent fraud, the ad SDK may automatically process technical information such as IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), device make and model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app version, SDK-generated identifiers such as the app set ID (not the Advertising ID), and ad interaction events (for example that an ad was shown, closed or clicked). With these settings this information is meant to be used only for contextual (non-personalised) ads, frequency capping, reporting, security and fraud prevention, not to build advertising profiles. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login screens. We do not ask anyone for personal information.
  • No chat, no friend lists and no user-generated content.
  • No in-app purchases and no real-money payments.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise (GPS) location.
  • No in-game web browser. The Privacy Policy button in Settings opens this page in your device's browser, and tapping an ad may open Google Play or the advertiser's page.
  • No sale or rental of data, no interest-based (behavioural) advertising and no remarketing.

6. Age of players and children's privacy

Bharat Blocks is a general puzzle game for people aged 13 and over. Its content is family-friendly, but it is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country).

The child-directed ad settings in section 4 are a precaution applied to every player. They do not mean the game is made for children.

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, United States): the game is not directed at children under 13, it does not ask for personal information, and every ad request carries the child-directed tag.
  • EU and UK GDPR: the game does not ask for personal data, and every ad request is tagged as under the age of consent and non-personalised. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to handle your request.
  • India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023: this Act treats anyone under 18 as a child. The game has no accounts, does not ask players of any age for personal data, and we do no behavioural tracking or targeted advertising.

7. For parents: if a child under 13 used Bharat Blocks

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used Bharat Blocks, or has sent us personal information, please email sarathalapad@gmail.com with the subject "Child under 13 - Bharat Blocks". We will:

  • tell you what we hold. This is normally nothing, unless the child emailed us;
  • delete any such email or other information within 30 days; and
  • explain how to remove the game and its on-device data.

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on the device and stops its connectivity checks and ad requests. We have no accounts and do not receive the data processed by the ad SDK, so we cannot look up a device or a child. That data is held by Google, under the Google Privacy Policy linked in section 4.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings. Uninstalling removes all of it.
  • We hold no game data or personal data on any server. For any deletion request (for example an email you sent us), email sarathalapad@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bharat Blocks data"; we act on it within 30 days.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob for fraud prevention and reporting is handled under Google's own retention rules.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change adds new kinds of data collection, we will also update the Google Play listing and, where required, ask for consent first.
