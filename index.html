Bharat Blocks Privacy Policy

Sarathkumar.K · Effective date: 28 October 2026

Summary

  • Bharat Blocks is a block puzzle game for children and families. You do not need an account, a login, an email address or a phone number to play.
  • The game does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.
  • Your progress, settings and chosen language are saved only on your device.
  • The game needs an internet connection to play. It checks the connection with a small request to a public internet address, and pauses while the device is offline.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are configured as child-directed, non-personalised and limited to a G (general audience) rating. The game does not collect the Android Advertising ID.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data for advertising profiles.

1. Who we are

Bharat Blocks (Android package com.bharat.blocks) is developed and published by Sarathkumar.K ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about your child's data, contact us at sarathalapad@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps the following on your device only: high score, Adventure progress (levels and regions unlocked, stars), settings (such as sound, music and vibration) and your chosen language. This data is never sent to us or to any server. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it.

3. Internet connection check

Bharat Blocks requires an internet connection. While the game is running it makes a simple HTTP request to a public internet address to find out whether the device is online. If there is no connection, the game pauses and asks you to reconnect. This request carries no personal information and no game data. Like any internet request, the server that answers it receives the device's IP address and basic technical request information. We do not store or use the result for anything other than deciding whether the device is online.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bharat Blocks is free and is supported by ads delivered through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, by Google LLC), which takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. No other ad network or mediation SDK is used.

How ads are configured for children

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA "tag for child-directed treatment") and as under the age of consent, and are non-personalised.
  • The maximum ad content rating is set to G (general audiences) on every ad request.
  • The game removes the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission, so the Advertising ID is not collected.

What the ad SDK may process

To deliver ads, count impressions and prevent fraud, the ad SDK may automatically process technical information such as IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), device make and model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app version, and ad interaction events (for example that an ad was shown, closed or clicked). In child-directed mode this information is used only for contextual (non-personalised) ads, frequency capping, reporting, security and fraud prevention, not to build advertising profiles. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login screens. We do not ask children (or anyone) for personal information.
  • No chat, no friend lists and no user-generated content.
  • No in-app purchases and no real-money payments.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise (GPS) location.
  • No sale or rental of data, no interest-based (behavioural) advertising and no remarketing.

6. Children's privacy

Bharat Blocks is designed for children and families. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children's data, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and Google Play's Families policies, by not collecting personal information from children in the game and by configuring all ads as child-directed, non-personalised and rated G. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us at sarathalapad@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings. Uninstalling removes all of it.
  • We hold no game data or personal data on any server. For any deletion request (for example an email you sent us), email sarathalapad@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bharat Blocks data"; we act on it within 30 days.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob for fraud prevention and reporting is handled under Google's own retention rules.

8. Parents' rights

Parents and guardians can ask us at any time what information is held about their child, ask us to delete it, and ask us to stop any further processing, by emailing sarathalapad@gmail.com. You can also reset the device's ad settings and uninstall the game at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change adds new kinds of data collection, we will also update the Google Play listing and, where required, ask for consent first.
